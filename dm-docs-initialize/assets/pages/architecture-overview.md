## Decision records

Decisions live in `adrs/` as `adr-NNN-kebab-title.md`, three digits, sequential. A decision
is a constraint plus one verifiable consequence. Where the running system knowingly departs
from a decision, the departure is registered in [Exceptions](exceptions.md).
